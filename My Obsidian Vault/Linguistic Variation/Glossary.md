**Area-class map:** Area-class maps depict phenomena or objects which are extensively distributed. Imhof ([1972](http://www.gitta.info/ThematicCart/en/html/ThematicCart_bibliography.html#d9e1355), p. 115) distinguishes three types:  
- **Truly areal areas**: e.g. rocks, waters, glaciated areas, forest areas and areas of arable lands, etc.
- **Fictitious areas**: e.g. areas with political or legal properties, areas of influence, etc.
- **Areas as a form of generalisation of scattered and isolated single objects**: e.g. limits of human occurrence, occurrence of animals, plants, diseases, etc.
